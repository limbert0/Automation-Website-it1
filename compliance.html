Compliance

For the person who owns the risk.

The duties sit with the organisation that controls the data. We design the system so the duties that apply to it can be met.

What GDPR compliance means on a build

The UK GDPR principles are not a checklist at the end. They are decisions made while the system is designed.

  • Data protection by design and by default

    Privacy settings and limits are the default state of the system, not an option someone has to find and switch on.

  • Lawful basis and purpose

    The system only processes personal data for the purpose the organisation has named. It does not grow new uses quietly.

  • Data minimisation

    Fields that are not required for the job are left out of the design. The least data that works is the data that is held.

  • Storage limitation

    Retention is part of the design: what is kept, for how long, and what is removed when the period ends.

  • Integrity and confidentiality

    Encryption at rest and in transit, named accounts, least-privilege access, and a log of who opened what.

  • Accountability

    Documentation your data protection officer can review: where personal data sits, who can reach it, and how the AI system works.

What we will not say
We do not claim certificates we do not hold — not ISO 27001, not Cyber Essentials, not FCA or SRA regulated. What we do claim is that every application is designed to meet UK GDPR, and the evidence is written down on each job.
