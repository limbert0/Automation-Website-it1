Compliance and security

GDPR compliant, by design.

Every application we build is designed to be compliant with UK GDPR from the first design decision — not patched on afterwards. Data protection by design and by default is how the system is put together, and the evidence is written down so you can check it, not just take our word for it.

  • UK GDPR by design

    Personal data is mapped before a line of code is written: what is collected, why, where it sits, who can see it, and when it is removed. Every application we deliver is built to be GDPR compliant.

  • In-house inference

    Where possible the model runs on infrastructure you control — your machines, or one kept for you alone. Records never pass through a public AI tool.

  • Data minimisation

    The system asks for the least it needs. Fields that are not required for the job are left out of the design, not just hidden from the screen.

  • Access and audit

    Named accounts, least-privilege access, and a log of who opened what. You can read the log, and you can remove access.

  • UK residency

    Data is held in the UK wherever the work allows it. Nothing crosses a border without being named in the contract first.

  • Documentation you can hand to a DPO

    Where personal data sits, who can reach it, and how the AI system works — written down so your data protection officer can review it, not just take our word for it.

On every job you receive: a map of where personal data sits, the named access list, a readable audit log, and the retention schedule. Your data protection officer can review all four.

How we work

A short engagement with a clear shape. Most work runs in weeks, not quarters, and each step produces a document you own — nothing about your system lives only in our heads.

  • Discover

    We look at the records, the task, and the rules that apply to them. You get a written scope.

  • Design

    The system is designed around your data boundary first, the interface second. You get a design you can challenge.

  • Build

    We build in the open on your infrastructure. You can see progress, and nothing is a black box.

  • Test

    Security testing before delivery: what can leave, who can reach it, and where it breaks. You get the written result.

  • Handover

    You run it, change who has access, and read the log. We remain on call if you want us.
